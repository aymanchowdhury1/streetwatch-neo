Register each ESP32 separately. Each device connects outward to this public HTTPS website, so devices and your phone can use different Wi-Fi networks. Never use localhost for a remotely hosted device.

Phone setup: Flash the updated firmware once. Hold the ESP32 SETUP button (GPIO4 to GND) for 4 seconds, connect your phone to STREETWATCH-SETUP, then open http://192.168.4.1. The WPA2 password is SWneo- plus the last 6 characters of the ESP32 MAC address. Enter Wi-Fi, server URL, device ID and token.

A device token is displayed only once after registration or rotation. Copy it directly into the ESP32 setup portal and store it securely. It is never shown in the device list.
